# About

Apex Genetics documents a personal cannabis breeding program: goals, methods, results and the plant library behind it.

Entries are written in Markdown through the [admin panel](/admin/). Set `type:` in an entry's front matter to one of `challenges`, `tutorials`, `grow-reports`, `breeding-reports`, `plans`, `seed-stash`, `mothers` or `cuts`.
